LEARN SIGN

Your hands can talk. Let's teach them how.

Start with the alphabet and numbers, then move into the grammar that makes signing feel natural — including the face and body cues that carry half the meaning.

Alphabet & Numbers Videos
01 · INTERACTIVE

Alphabet & numbers

Tap any letter or number to see how the handshape is formed. This is fingerspelling — the backbone you fall back on for names, brands, and any word you don't yet know the sign for.

FINGERSPELLING
A

Sign for "A"

Closed fist, thumb rests along the side.

  • Keep your dominant hand in a comfortable "signing space" near your shoulder.
  • Palm usually faces the person you're signing to.
  • Pause briefly between Numbers/Letters — don't rush.
