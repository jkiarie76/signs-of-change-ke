ABOUT ME

Hi, I’m Jeridah — writer, signer, and reluctant explainer.

I grew up in a mixed hearing–Deaf family, learned to sign before I learned to read, and started Hands & Voices because most of what the internet said about Deaf people was written by hearing people. I wanted a place with room for both.

You’ll find me in the comments, the newsletter, and — every other Friday — behind the camera for our short video letters.

MISSION

To tell Deaf stories in the language they deserve — and invite hearing readers in without dumbing anything down.

Language-first

Sign languages are full languages. We write about them that way — with grammar, culture, and history intact.

Access as design

Captions, transcripts, alt text, plain language, and high-contrast type are the baseline — not an add-on.

Community-led

Deaf writers, Deaf editors, Deaf sensitivity readers. Hearing allies welcomed, never centered.
