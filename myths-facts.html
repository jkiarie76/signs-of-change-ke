Debunking Misconceptions

Exploring reality vs. fiction in deaf culture, communication access, and visual language systems.

Myth

"Sign Language is completely universal across the entire globe."

The Fact

There are over 300 distinct sign languages worldwide. For example, Kenyan Sign Language (KSL) has completely different grammatical patterns, vocabulary traits, and structural origins than British Sign Language (BSL).

Myth

"Deaf individuals can easily read lips with 100% precision."

The Fact

Lip-reading is largely a guessing game. Only about 30% to 40% of English or Swahili spoken sounds are visually clear on the lips. The rest relies heavily on contextual reasoning, physical gestures, and facial grammar indicators.

Myth

"All deaf individuals consider their hearing loss a severe medical handicap."

The Fact

The vast majority of the community views being deaf not as a medical pathology to fix, but as a distinct linguistic culture. They take pride in their unique history, social networks, and values.
